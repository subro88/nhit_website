### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in Machine Design, Thermal Engineering & Precision Manufacturing**

#### Message from the Head of Department

Mechanical Engineering remains the evergreen cornerstone of industrial civilization. At NHIT, our objective is to instill analytical rigor, digital prototyping capability, and fearless shop-floor mastery in every aspiring diploma engineer.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Graduates recruited by L&T, John Deere, TIL India and the wider plant & machinery industry around Durgapur; strong ladder into B.Tech lateral entry.
