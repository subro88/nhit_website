### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in Wireless Communications, Microcontrollers, Embedded Systems & Signal Processing**

#### Message from the Head of Department

The rapid evolution of 5G, satellite telemetry, and embedded smart IoT devices relies on modern electronics engineers. Our ETCE program unites deep circuit simulation with rigorous RF testing, digital signal processing, and programmable FPGA/ARM microcontroller design.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Alumni placed across electronics manufacturing, telecom field operations and service industries; embedded-systems projects featured at the annual project expo.
