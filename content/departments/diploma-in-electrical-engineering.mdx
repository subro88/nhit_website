### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in Electrical Machines, Power Transmission, Smart Switchgear & Solar Photovoltaics**

#### Message from the Head of Department

From heavy power transmission grids to modern rooftop solar microgrids and industrial variable frequency drives, the world runs on electrical energy. At NHIT, we combine robust electrical machine laboratory work with advanced protection relays, transmission simulators, and solar technology to shape competent, safety-conscious electrical engineers.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Graduates absorbed by steel & power sector contractors, electrical installers and plant maintenance teams across Paschim Bardhaman.
