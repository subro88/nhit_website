### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in Structural Analysis, Concrete Technology, Modern Surveying & Infrastructure Engineering**

#### Message from the Head of Department

Civil engineers leave their permanent signature upon civilization. Our department grounds students in both rigorous structural theory and authentic field practice—from precision digital Total Station surveys to automated concrete mix testing and computerized CAD drafting. We prepare site-ready engineers with uncompromising safety ethics.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Diploma holders join construction majors, PHE projects and survey teams; a proven route into B.Tech civil via lateral entry.
