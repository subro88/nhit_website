### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in IC Engines, Automotive Chassis Design, Electric Mobility & Diagnostics**

#### Message from the Head of Department

Automotive engineering blends thermal mechanical power with high-speed digital electronics and EV powertrain innovations. At NHIT, we immerse students in hands-on multi-cylinder engine overhaul, dynamometer testing, electronic OBD-II vehicle diagnostics, and electric vehicle battery systems.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Recruiters on record: Anand Group, L&T, John Deere, JBM, TIL India, Sharda Motors, Tata Motors. Industry association cited with Tata Motors, Cummins India, Maruti, Honda, JCB and Magna Automotive. 80–85% of interested, eligible students placed; 15–20% pursue higher studies.
