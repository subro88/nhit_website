### Department Overview

> **Center of Excellence in Software Systems, Cloud Computing, Full-Stack Engineering & Cyber Infrastructure**

#### Message from the Head of Department

Computing is the sole engineering discipline where you can architect, build, and deploy an enterprise solution directly from your terminal. At NHIT, our mission is to build robust algorithmic foundations, clean engineering practices, and fearless problem-solving abilities so that our diploma engineers excel in modern tech ecosystems and elite B.Tech lateral entry programs.

#### Career Outcomes & Placements

Graduates progress into high-demand software development, cloud systems, and database administration roles; the department also provides direct lateral pathway into elite B.Tech programs through JELET.
