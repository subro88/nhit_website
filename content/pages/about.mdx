<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>New Horizons Institute of Technology (NHIT)</strong> opened its first academic session on 16 August 2007 at Durgapur, Paschim Bardhaman. Today it is one of eastern India's largest diploma engineering institutes by student intake, running six diploma branches and a Private ITI with Electrician and Fitter trades.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The first pass-out batch set the tone: <strong>85% of that batch moved into jobs or B.Tech lateral entry</strong> — a record the institute has worked to keep through industry MoUs, workshop-first teaching and an active placement cell.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
